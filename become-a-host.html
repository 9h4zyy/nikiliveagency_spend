Niki Live Host Withdrawal Information & Guidelines

  • • 10,000 Beans = 1 USD
  • • Minimum single withdrawal amount: $10 USD
  • • Reward is calculated based on the last 7 days earnings, this means today + the previous 6 days.
  • • When the daily earnings increase to a higher level, the daily reward will also increase
  • • If a host registers multiple accounts, including but not limited to using multiple devices, all related rewards will be cancelled.
  • • Hosts can request withdrawals at any time, but Niki processes payments once a week.
  • • Service fees vary depending on the selected withdrawal method.
  • • Niki supports multiple withdrawal options, including: Epay, Payoneer, PayPal, and bank transfer.
