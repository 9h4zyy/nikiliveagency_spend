Essential Niki Live Rules for Hosts

Every Niki Live host must follow the platform rules to maintain a safe, professional, and high-quality livestream environment.

Registration Rules

  • Hosts are not allowed to register multiple accounts.
  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content is prohibited.
  • Promoting third-party platforms is not permitted.
  • Minors are not allowed to livestream on Niki Live.

Live Cover Photo Rules

  • Only hosts with approved cover photos can appear on the live page.
  • Your livestream cover photo should look attractive and presentable.
  • Use a clean and bright background for your livestream.
  • Choose a high-quality upper-body photo instead of a face-only or head-only image.

Niki Live Host Guidelines for Better Livestreaming

A good livestream experience begins before you go live and continues even after the session ends. Follow these practical guidelines to improve presentation and audience interaction.

01

Before Livestream

  • Prepare a clean and tidy livestream background.
  • Make sure your upper body is visible instead of showing only your face or head.
  • Prepare your appearance before starting the livestream.
  • Avoid low-quality setups, such as livestreaming while lying down or pointing the camera at an empty area.
02

During Livestream

  • Check your livestream quality periodically.
  • Interact actively with viewers by greeting them, starting conversations, and thanking them.
  • Encourage viewers to follow your account or send gifts when appropriate.
03

After Livestream

  • Stay connected with viewers who send gifts to maintain good relationships.
  • Share interesting livestream highlights on the community page to attract more traffic and engagement.

How to Complete Face Verification as a Niki Live Host

Face verification is part of the host verification process. Follow the steps below to submit your verification correctly.

1

Start the Verification Process

Go to the Niki Live homepage and click “Start Live.”

2

Select Verify Now

Click “Verify Now” to begin the face verification process.

3

Follow the Instructions

Complete the face verification by following the instructions shown on the screen.

4

Wait for Verification

Once submitted, your verification will be reviewed. The process may take up to 3 business days.

How to Withdraw Your Niki Live Earnings

Once your account is verified and active, you can access your Beans balance and submit a withdrawal request through your Niki Live account.

1

Open Your Profile

Log in to your host or agent account and open the profile section.

Notes: Your account must be verified and active as a host before you can receive your balance.
2

Open the Beans Menu

  • Click the “Beans” menu.
  • Click “Recharge.”
  • Select “Withdraw.”
  • Choose your preferred withdrawal method. Available methods listed in the source include:
    • Bank Transfer
    • PayPal
    • Epay
    • Binance
    • USDT
    • G-Cash
    • E-wallet
    Availability may differ depending on your country.
3

Enter Your Payout Information

Provide the required information for your chosen withdrawal method. Make sure all payout information is correct. Incorrect details may cause the withdrawal to fail.

Next:
  • Choose how many Beans you want to exchange into USD.
  • The source states a minimum payout of 2,000,000 Beans, or approximately $200 USD. This amount may change.
  • Review the information and click “Submit.”
