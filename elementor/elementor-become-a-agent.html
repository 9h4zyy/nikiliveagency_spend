2

3

4

Submit Agency Registration

5

Requirements to Become a Niki Live Agent

Successful agents help build an active host community while maintaining quality standards and supporting their team's growth.

  • Build an Active Host Team Maintain at least 10 active hosts under your agency.
  • Support Host Activity Each host should stream at least 1 hour per day and 4 days per week.
  • Achieve Agency Performance Targets Reach a minimum agency income target of 500,000 Beans every two weeks.
  • Manage Your Team Professionally Guide hosts, monitor performance, and help create quality live streaming content.

Niki Live Agency Rules

All Niki Live agents must follow platform guidelines to maintain a safe, fair, and professional live streaming environment.

Anti-Poaching Policy

Agents cannot recruit hosts who are currently registered under another Niki Live agency. Violation consequences may include:

  1. First violation: $10 penalty.
  2. Second violation: $100 penalty
  3. Repeated violations: Commission cancellation and agency termination.

Multi-Account & Promotion Rules

Agents and hosts are prohibited from:

  1. Promoting competing applications on Niki Live
  2. Joining multiple agencies simultaneously
  3. Creating multiple accounts to manipulate agency systems

Host Payment & Exchange Policy

Verified complaints regarding unfair payment practices may result in commission restrictions. Agent must:

  1. Pay hosts fairly
  2. Avoid unauthorized deductions
  3. Follow approved exchange rate requirements

Agency Management Responsibility

Agents are held responsible for

  1. Maintaining good relationships with hosts
  2. Managing host activities
  3. Ensuring compliance with Niki Live guidelines

Agency Growth Rewards

Growth Policy 01 / 04

Reward Eligibility & Claim Rules

  • Rewards are only unlocked when both the required number of eligible hosts and the shareable performance targets are met simultaneously.
  • Rewards earned this week are collected independently the following week.
  • Only the current level of rewards can be claimed each week.

Eligible Host Criteria (“Up to Standard”)

To count as 1 eligible host per week, the host must meet all of the following:

  • Minimum of 1 hour of broadcasting per day.
  • Minimum of 6 valid days per week.
  • Minimum of 15 total hours of broadcasting time per week.

Agent Ranking & Tier Limitations

  • Level 8–10 Tasks: Restricted exclusively to agents ranked in the Top 14 by income this week.
  • Level 0–7 Tasks: Mandatory for any agents who did not rank in the Top 14 last week.

Fraud & Content Quality Penalties

  • Fraudulent Behavior: Any discovered fraud will result in the immediate cancellation of event rewards and disqualification from future participation.
  • Host Quality: Agencies will lose all growth rewards if their hosts produce low-quality content and fail to improve after multiple warnings.

Build Your Network with Niki Live Sub-agents

A sub-agent works under your main agency account while operating independently with the same rights, responsibilities, and opportunities as the main agent.

Benefits of Sub-agents

  • Sub-agent revenue contributes to your overall weekly income calculation.
  • A stronger agency network can help you achieve higher commission tiers faster.

Important Notes

  • Sub-agent commission depends on their own performance.
  • Main agency bonuses from sub-agents are provided directly by Niki Live.
  • Sub-agent earnings are not reduced.
